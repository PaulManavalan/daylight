# Training workspace

The `/day/fitness` checkpoint provides browser-local routines, explicitly chosen planned sessions, and actual workout logs. It supports soccer, running, strength, calisthenics, mobility and other activities. Exercise records accept optional sets, reps, time and external resistance; running records accept distance and show pace calculated from actual recorded distance and duration.

Planning a routine copies a snapshot. Only sessions explicitly included in the daily plan become flexible training blocks, kept whole around protected commitments. Unavailable sessions are deferred visibly. Logging completion requires review and actual duration; planned exercise quantities never become performance records automatically. Deleting a linked completion restores the planned session. Changes to selected training invalidate an existing day plan while preserving the conversation.

Calendar export remains focus-only by default. A separate approved-plan export includes chosen training. Neither export writes directly to a provider or marks training completed.

Equipment and self-reported restriction preferences are editable. Local private profile suggestions require review and explicit saving. No personalized training prescription, automatic progression, calorie expenditure, body-weight tracking, cloud synchronization or coach-program reconciliation is implemented. A generated coaching feature needs the user's existing team and school workload first.

Educational references: [AOSSM youth strength training](https://www.sportsmed.org/youth-strength-training), [AAP overuse injury prevention](https://www.healthychildren.org/English/health-issues/injuries-emergencies/sports-injuries/Pages/Preventing-Overuse-Injuries.aspx), and [AAOS flexibility](https://www.orthoinfo.org/staying-healthy/fitness--exercise/flexibility-exercises-for-young-athletes/). These inform conservative product boundaries; they are not personalized exercise clearance.
